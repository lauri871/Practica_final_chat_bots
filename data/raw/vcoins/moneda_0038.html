EF Trajan Ptolemais Galilee AE24 Tyche Seated River God Swimming Below

Trajan, 25 January 98 - 8 or 9 August 117 A.D., Ptolemais, Galilee, Syria Palestina
 
RP116984. Bronze AE 24, RPC III 3911; Sofaer 135; BMC Phoenicia p. 132, 19; Hendin 818; Kadman 97; Baramki AUB 13; Rosenberger 47; SNG Cop -, EF, excellent portrait, dark blue-green patina, light red-brown earthen deposits, surfaces slightly rough, 11.549g, 23.8mm, 0o, Galilee, Ake Ptolemais (Acre, Israel) mint, IMP CAES NER TRAIANO OPT AVG GER DAC PAR (Latin: Imperator Caesar Nervae Traiano Optimo Augusto Germanico Dacico Parthico - supreme commander, caesar, Nerva Trajan, the best emperor, victor over the Germans, victor over the Dacians, victor over the Parthians), laureate head right; reverse COL POTL (Latin: Colonia Ptolemais - colony of Ptolemais), Tyche seated right on rock, wearing veil and kalathos on head, stalks of grain downward in right hand, river god swimming right below; first specimen of this type handled by FORVM
 
Ptolemais was a maritime city of Galilee (Acts 21:7). It was originally Accho (or Akko), but was renamed Ptolemais under the rule of Ptolemy Soter.
