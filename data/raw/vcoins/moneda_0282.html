Severus Alexander Silver Denarius 231-5 AD Mars in Military Garb Walking Right

Severus Alexander, 13 March 222 - March 235 A.D.
 
RS119251. Silver denarius, RSC III 161a, BMCRE VI 831, RIC IV 246, SRCV 7882, Hunter III -, Choice VF, well centered, frosty surfaces, flow lines, light deposits, 2.247g, 19.0mm, 180o, Rome mint, IMP ALEXANDER PIVS AVG (Latin: Imperator Alexander Pius Augustus - supreme commander, Alexander Pius, emperor), laureate, draped, and cuirassed bust right, seen from the front; reverse MARS VLTOR (Latin: Mars the avenger), Mars walking right in military garb, spear transverse in right hand, oval shield in left hand
 
Two temples were dedicated to 'Mars the Avenger' during the reign of Augustus: the first in 20 B.C. on the Capitol for the standards recovered from the Parthians, the second in 2 B.C. as a memorial to Julius Caesar.
